About Us

Welcome to Corporate Majdoor. We are an online music streaming utility designed to help modern professionals, IT workers, and corporate employees maintain focus and productivity during their daily hustle.

Our Purpose

Corporate Majdoor provides a curated ambient music experience. Our visual player creates the perfect environment for stress relief and focus by blending lo-fi beats, subtle office background noise, and calming melodies.

How It Works

Our website utilizes the official YouTube embedded player to stream music. We act as a curated interface over legitimate, public third-party content. We do not host, store, or distribute any music or audio files on our servers.

What We Provide (and Don't Provide)

  • We Provide: A distraction-free visual interface to play ambient music via YouTube.
  • We Do Not Provide: MP3 downloads, audio extraction, or unauthorized access to copyrighted materials.

Third-Party Content

All music streamed on our website remains the property of its respective owners and is subject to the terms and policies of YouTube. Corporate Majdoor does not claim any ownership over the audio content provided through the YouTube player.
