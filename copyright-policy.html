Copyright & DMCA Policy

Respect for Intellectual Property

Corporate Majdoor deeply respects the intellectual property rights of artists, creators, and record labels. We do not host, upload, or store any copyrighted audio or video files on our servers.

How Our Service Works

All multimedia content on our website is streamed directly from YouTube using YouTube's official embedded player API. The videos remain hosted on YouTube's servers, and YouTube handles all licensing, monetization, and copyright enforcement for that content.

Filing a Copyright Complaint

Because the content is hosted on YouTube, the most effective way to have copyrighted content removed is to submit a takedown request directly to YouTube. Once a video is removed or restricted by YouTube, it will automatically stop working on our website.

If you believe that our website interface is infringing on your rights in a way separate from the YouTube embed, please contact us via our Contact page with:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Your contact information.
