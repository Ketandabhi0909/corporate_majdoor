Music & Content Policy

Strictly a Streaming Interface

Corporate Majdoor is a curated streaming interface designed to provide ambient background music. We operate strictly within legal boundaries by utilizing official third-party embedding tools.

What We Do NOT Allow

To remain compliant with copyright laws and platform policies, our website explicitly DOES NOT:

  • Host any MP3, WAV, or audio files.
  • Provide tools to extract audio from videos.
  • Offer YouTube-to-MP3 conversion services.
  • Allow unauthorized downloading or offline saving of copyrighted music.
  • Circumvent the official YouTube player or hide mandatory YouTube branding.

Any attempt by users to exploit our interface to illegally download content is strictly prohibited and violates our Terms of Service.
