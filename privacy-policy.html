Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Corporate Majdoor ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the basic music streaming functionality. However, we may collect technical information automatically when you visit, including:

  • Log data and device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (time spent on page, interaction with the player)

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our website utilizes third-party services that may collect data about your interaction:

  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • YouTube: Our music player embeds content from YouTube. YouTube may collect data according to their own Privacy Policy.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use cookies and local browser storage to enhance your experience (e.g., tracking anonymous concurrent active viewers for our live counter). Third-party vendors like Google also use cookies to serve ads.

4. Data Retention and Security

We only retain collected technical data for as long as necessary to provide our services. We implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access to our systems.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact page.
