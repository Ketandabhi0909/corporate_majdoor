YouTube Embed Policy

Compliance with YouTube API Services

Corporate Majdoor utilizes the official YouTube IFrame Player API to provide playback of ambient and office music content. Our implementation is designed to fully comply with YouTube's Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

No Circumvention

We do not circumvent or modify the standard behavior of the YouTube player. We do not block ads served by YouTube within the player, nor do we hide YouTube branding or links back to the original creators.

No Downloads

In accordance with YouTube's policies, our service does not separate, isolate, or modify the audio or video components of any YouTube content. We do not permit or facilitate the downloading of YouTube videos or audio streams.

Not an Official Partnership

While we use the official YouTube embed tools, we are an independent website and do not claim any official partnership, approval, or endorsement by YouTube or Google LLC.
